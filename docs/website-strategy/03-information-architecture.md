# Information Architecture

## Positioning Statement

Prof. Saeed Ahmed Khan is an engineering academic and materials-sensing researcher advancing flexible, wearable, self-powered, and thermogalvanic sensing systems while leading engineering education, accreditation, and quality-enhancement initiatives at Sukkur IBA University.

## Primary Audiences

- International collaborators seeking research alignment.
- Graduate students and prospective supervisees.
- Funding agencies and research councils.
- University leadership, accreditation reviewers, and quality-assurance bodies.
- Industry R&D partners in sensors, energy harvesting, and electronic materials.
- Journal editors, conference organizers, and reviewers.
- Search committees and academic visitors.

## Content Pillars

1. Flexible and wearable electronics.
2. Self-powered sensing and triboelectric nanogenerators.
3. Thermogalvanic hydrogels and energy conversion.
4. Carbon nanomaterials, thin films, printed electronics, and EHD fabrication.
5. Wearable health, respiration, posture, facial-expression, agriculture, and HMI applications.
6. Engineering education leadership, accreditation, OBE, and quality assurance.
7. International collaboration, supervision, and research training.

## Sitemap

- Home: identity, metrics, research themes, selected papers, leadership, collaboration map/timeline, CTA.
- About: professional profile, career timeline, education, industry grounding, memberships, skills.
- Research: thematic clusters, applications, methods, related publications.
- Publications: searchable/filterable publication system with featured papers, DOI links, copy citation, BibTeX.
- Projects and Grants: funded projects, grant roles, themes, institutional research building.
- Teaching and Supervision: courses, supervision summary, mentoring philosophy, prospective student/collaborator note.
- Leadership and Service: dean role, QEC, accreditation, rankings, governance, editorial/reviewer service.
- Collaborations: international experience, institutions, collaborator context supported by CV.
- News: maintainable updates based on CV facts and recent publications.
- Contact: official contact card, email, phone, address, public academic links.

## SEO And Metadata

Every page should include:

- Specific page title.
- Concise meta description.
- Academic keywords.
- Open Graph title and description.
- Shared academic site metadata.

Structured data:

- JSON-LD `Person` schema on the site layout or home page.
- `affiliation`, `jobTitle`, `worksFor`, `alumniOf`, `email`, `telephone`, and `sameAs` links where supported.

## Content Traceability

All public claims should map to one of:

- Latest CV.
- Older resume used as supporting source.
- Public peer research used only for design pattern decisions.

Any claim not in the CV should be omitted or recorded in `07-fact-check-notes.md`.
