# Peer Website Research

## Method

Research was sampled from official professor, lab, institute, and university pages in flexible electronics, wearable bioelectronics, nanogenerators, soft materials, bioelectronics, and translational research. The goal is not to copy layouts, but to identify durable information patterns for an academic identity website for Prof. Saeed Ahmed Khan.

## Cross-Site Synthesis

### Navigation

The strongest sites separate:

- Home or overview.
- About / professor profile.
- Research themes.
- Publications.
- Projects, grants, and impact.
- Teaching, students, or openings.
- Leadership/service where relevant.
- News or updates.
- Contact.

For Prof. Khan, a multi-page structure is justified because the CV spans research, teaching, grants, quality assurance, accreditation, editorial work, and international collaboration.

### Homepage

Effective academic homepages do four things quickly:

- Establish identity and institutional role.
- State the research domain in one sentence.
- Show proof through selected publications, metrics, grants, or news.
- Route different audiences to the right next page.

The homepage for Prof. Khan should balance a dean-level executive identity with the technical specificity of flexible electronics and self-powered sensing.

### Research Grouping

Leading labs do not list every keyword. They group work into named themes. Recommended clusters:

- Flexible carbon-nanomaterial sensors.
- Thermogalvanic hydrogels and self-powered biosensing.
- Triboelectric nanogenerators and hybrid energy harvesting.
- Printed and soft electronics, including EHD fabrication.
- Wearable health, HMI, agriculture, respiration, posture, and facial-expression applications.
- Engineering systems and sustainable energy.

### Publications

Best practice:

- Provide featured publications for first-time visitors.
- Provide full searchable/filterable lists for specialists.
- Include year, authors, venue, DOI/link, type, and theme.
- Keep citation and BibTeX actions close to each item.
- Avoid unsupported citation metrics, journal impact factors, or ranking claims.

### People And Alumni

Lab sites highlight members and alumni, but Prof. Khan's CV does not provide public student names. The site should show supervision summaries and research areas, not invented people profiles.

### News And Impact

News works when it is current and maintainable. Since only CV-based updates are available, the initial news page should use a small data model with verifiable updates: Dean appointment, SRSP grant, recent 2024-2025 papers, QEC role completion, and SKKU postdoctoral work.

### Visual Identity

The strongest relevant visual languages use:

- Research images or abstracted materials imagery.
- Thin technical lines and grids.
- Clear typography and institutional restraint.
- Cards for research areas and papers.
- Minimal animation.

Prof. Khan's site should use an original identity based on flexible traces, hydrogel transparency, nanoscale grids, and academic navy/cyan contrast.

### What To Avoid

- A generic Bootstrap academic template.
- A single-page resume dump.
- Unsupported "world-renowned" or "pioneer" language.
- Fake lab members, student names, patent lists, media coverage, or h-index.
- Overly animated science effects that distract from credibility.
- Contact pages without official institutional context.
