# Page-By-Page Blueprint

## Home

Purpose: establish identity, research authority, and leadership scope quickly.

Sections:

- Hero with name, current role, research positioning, official affiliation, CV/publication/contact buttons, and professional portrait slot.
- Metrics from CV: citations, publications, years, countries, theses, review activity.
- Research theme cards.
- Featured publications from 2024-2025 and foundational papers.
- Leadership and quality-assurance impact.
- International experience timeline/map.
- Audience CTAs: collaborators, prospective students, industry/accreditation bodies.
- Latest updates.

## About

Purpose: provide a polished executive academic biography.

Sections:

- Profile summary.
- Career timeline.
- Education.
- Industrial grounding.
- Memberships, languages, and technical capabilities.

## Research

Purpose: convert CV keywords into coherent research clusters.

Clusters:

- Flexible Sensors Based on Carbon Nanomaterials.
- Thermogalvanic Hydrogels and Self-Powered Biosensing.
- Triboelectric Nanogenerators and Hybrid Energy Harvesting.
- Printed and Soft Electronics with EHD Fabrication.
- Wearable Health, HMI, Respiration, Posture, Facial Expression, and Agriculture.
- Sustainable Energy and Engineering Systems.

Each cluster includes:

- Plain-English summary.
- Technical keywords.
- Application domains.
- Selected related publications.

## Publications

Purpose: give researchers a useful publication interface.

Features:

- Featured publication grid.
- Search by title, author, venue, and year.
- Filter by year, theme, and type.
- DOI and external links.
- Copy citation.
- Copy BibTeX.
- Counts by type/year.

## Projects And Grants

Purpose: show funded research and institutional research building.

Sections:

- Grants timeline.
- Funding role cards.
- Research capacity themes: flexible sensors, BCI/EEG, sustainable energy, sensor materials.
- Traceability to CV grant descriptions.

## Teaching And Supervision

Purpose: show teaching breadth and mentoring scope without inventing student data.

Sections:

- Supervision summary.
- Selected courses grouped by electronics, sensors/materials, controls/instrumentation, computing/HCI.
- Mentoring statement grounded in research areas.
- Professional call for prospective students/collaborators.

## Leadership And Service

Purpose: present dean/QEC/editor/reviewer roles with executive polish.

Sections:

- Dean role.
- QEC and national certified reviewer role.
- Accreditation, OBE, rankings, evidence portfolios.
- Governance committees.
- Editorial and conference leadership.
- Reviewer service grouped by journals, conferences, and fellowships.

## Collaborations

Purpose: show international research mobility and collaboration context.

Sections:

- Country/institution timeline: Pakistan, China, South Korea, Croatia/EU, China collaborator network.
- Supported collaborator references: UESTC, SKKU, University of Zagreb, Taiyuan University of Technology.
- Research interface areas.

## News

Purpose: maintainable updates based on CV facts.

Initial updates:

- Dean appointment, January 2026.
- Recent 2025 and 2024 publications.
- SRSP 2024 grant.
- QEC leadership period completed January 2026.
- SKKU research period and outputs.

## Contact

Purpose: official academic contact and routing.

Sections:

- Structured contact card.
- Email, phone from CV, department address.
- Google Scholar and Scopus.
- Collaboration, supervision, and editorial/service inquiry notes.
