The 16th Meeting of the Senate of Sukkur IBA University was held on 30 December 2025 under the leadership of Vice Chancellor Prof. Dr. Asif Ahmed Shaikh, presiding on behalf of the Chancellor and Pro-Chancellor. The Senate reviewed key academic, administrative, and financial matters and expressed satisfaction over the University’s overall progress. Members highly appreciated the Vice Chancellor and his team for strengthening academic quality, governance, financial sustainability, and the University’s national and international standing over the past six months. The Senate approved the actuals for FY 2024–25, revised estimates for FY 2025–26 (Ghotki Campus), key statutes related to pay scales and service matters, and the Annual Report 2025, reaffirming confidence in Sukkur IBA University’s continued journey toward quality, transparency, and institutional excellence.
