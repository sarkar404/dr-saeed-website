The Board of Faculty (BoF) meeting of the Faculty of Engineering & Technology was held on 26 February 2026 at board room, Admin Block, Sukkur IBA University. The meeting was convened by Dr. Saeed Ahmed Khan, Dean, Faculty of Engineering & Technology.
During the meeting, the agenda items of the Department of Electrical Engineering and the Department of Computer Systems Engineering were presented for review. After deliberation, the Board endorsed the proposed academic matters and recommendations.
The meeting reflected the Faculty’s continued focus on strengthening academic quality and program development.
