The 23rd Syndicate Meeting of Sukkur IBA University was convened on 24 December 2025 under the leadership of the Chairman Syndicate and Vice Chancellor, Prof. Dr. Asif Ahmed Shaikh. The meeting was attended by the respected members of the Syndicate.
During the meeting, key academic, administrative, and policy-related matters were discussed in detail. Major agenda items included the consideration and approval of financial and establishment matters aimed at strengthening the University’s governance and operational efficiency. The University’s Annual Report was formally presented, highlighting academic achievements, financial performance, and developmental initiatives.
Furthermore, proposed statutes were presented, reviewed, and approved by the Syndicate. Several important decisions were taken to support the continued growth and strategic direction of Sukkur IBA University.
