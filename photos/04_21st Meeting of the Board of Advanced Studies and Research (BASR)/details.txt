Sukkur IBA University successfully convened the 21st Meeting of the Board of Advanced Studies and Research (BASR) on March 9, 2026, at the Main Campus Boardroom. The meeting was held under the leadership of the Honourable Vice Chancellor and Pro Vice Chancellor, who provided valuable guidance on enhancing academic quality, research governance, and postgraduate progress across all departments. During the session, the Board deliberated on several significant agenda items, including the strengthening of progress monitoring mechanisms for PhD, MS, and MPhil scholars, the streamlining of evaluation processes, the reinforcement of quality assurance and supervisory practices, the enhancement of interdepartmental coordination for student support and research facilitation, and the review of synopses, theses, and academic integrity protocols.
Sukkur IBA University remains steadfast in its commitment to promoting academic excellence, transparency, research integrity, and continuous improvement in postgraduate education.
