The Industrial Advisory Board (IAB) meeting of the Faculty of Engineering & Technology, Sukkur IBA University was successfully held on 3rd February 2026 at the Admin Block. The meeting was convened by Dr. Saeed Ahmed Khan Abro, Dean Faculty of Engineering & Technology, and brought together academic leadership and respected industry professionals through an online session.
During the meeting, the Board reviewed and discussed key improvements in the curricula of Bachelor of Engineering in Electrical Engineering (Electronics and Power) and Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Systems Engineering, ensuring stronger alignment with current industry requirements, emerging technologies, and future workforce skills.
A dedicated discussion was also held on integrating the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) within engineering education, emphasizing practical ways to embed sustainability into teaching, research, and future academic initiatives.
The convener sincerely thanked all participants and acknowledged the valuable suggestions and guidance shared by the IAB members. Their input will greatly support Sukkur IBA University in developing more skill-oriented, industry-ready, and future-focused engineering graduates.
