The Quality Enhancement Cell (QEC) of Sukkur IBA University held the Second meeting of the Institutional Quality Circle (IQC) on January 1st, 2026. The meeting was chaired by Prof. Dr. Asif Ahmed Shaikh, Vice-Chancellor, Sukkur IBA University, and facilitated by Dr. Saeed Ahmed Khan, Director QEC. 
Pro-Vice Chancellor (Main Campus) - Prof. Dr. Syed Mir Muhammad Shah, Deans, Heads of Departments, and the Pro-Vice Chancellor (Khairpur campus) – Prof. Dr Manzoor Ali Mirani attended the meeting.
Key agenda items included discussions on the upcoming Review of Institutional Performance and Enhancement (RIPE) visit by the Quality Assurance Agency (QAA), Higher Education Commission (HEC), scheduled next week. The forum reviewed institutional preparedness and quality compliance requirements.
The Online Teacher & Course Evaluation results (Fall-2025) were also presented and deliberated upon, emphasizing their role in continuous improvement of teaching and learning processes.
The Vice-Chancellor reaffirmed the University’s commitment to quality assurance, academic excellence, and continuous institutional improvement. 
The meeting concluded with a vote of thanks.
